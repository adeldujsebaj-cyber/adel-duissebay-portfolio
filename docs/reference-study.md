## Academic portfolio reference

- Supplied page: https://sites.google.com/view/abauto-ba/porfolio?authuser=0
- The accessible Google Sites wrapper embeds https://riaktor1.github.io/ba2026-portfolio/ through a whole-page embed.
- Inspected its overview, `deliverables.html`, `weekly-records.html` and `governance-sources.html`.
- Accessible structure includes a project overview, paired company profiles, clearly separated deliverables, nine weekly records, evidence links, sources and an academic document viewer. The deliverables page exposes original PDF, Word and PowerPoint links, an inline document-preview section and video embeds.
- Adapted ideas: direct record pages, connection between research text and original documents, prominent company pairing, explicit source links and previews with a download fallback.

## Comparative research reference

- Supplied page: https://sites.google.com/view/nuraydumankyzy/home
- Inspected accessible Home, About Me, Weekly Records, Artifacts, Attestation Syntheses, Capstone Project and Evidence Folder HTML.
- Navigation distinguishes student identity from research collections. The homepage introduces the project before two labeled company profiles. Weekly records run from 1 to 9; artifacts are grouped by week; attestation syntheses contain Assignment 1 through Assignment 4. Evidence Folder lists cited supporting sources.
- Document labels were accessible, but the full embedded assignment contents were not inspected. The accessible capstone page did not expose substantive presentation content; no hidden content or conclusions were inferred.
- Adapted ideas: a multipage navigation structure, W1–W9 records, weekly artifact slots, A1–A4 assignments, final capstone and searchable evidence library. No reference student identity, company text, data, findings or conclusions were transferred into the website.

## Visual authority

The user's palette image defines Deep Navy #101C2C, Royal Blue #1D3557, Champagne Gold #C5A46D, Warm Ivory #E8E6E1 and White #FFFFFF. The later example image defines the editorial composition: a utility bar, serif monogram, independent page links, spacious two-column identity hero, large serif name and an offset portrait frame. The implementation follows that composition using the supplied palette. The sample person's photograph and identity are not reused.

## Content boundaries

The owner will upload their own materials. Empty records show their actual upload state and contain no invented assignments, research results, numerical claims or personal achievements. Company introductions are short factual summaries linked directly to official sources:

- https://investor.bankofamerica.com/profile
- https://www.bankffin.kz/en/about

The original photo shown in chat was not exposed as a downloadable file or attachment ID in the execution environment. The dashboard provides protected original-image upload and replacement; no AI recreation of the person's image was used.
