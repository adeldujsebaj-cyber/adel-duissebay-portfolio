# Expanded company profiles

Sources checked on 10 November 2026. The originally supplied introductions and comparative perspective are preserved. Additional history, founder context and services are paraphrased from the following primary sources; no student research findings, numerical performance comparisons or financial recommendations have been added.

## Bank of America

- https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/our-company/celebrating250 — A. P. Giannini and Bank of Italy in 1904; the 1930 name; the 1998 merger; mobile banking in 2008; Erica in 2018.
- https://investor.bankofamerica.com/profile — Consumer, business, wealth-management and institutional services.
- The founder label explicitly refers to the historical Bank of Italy predecessor. The contemporary group also reflects the NationsBank–BankAmerica merger; Giannini is not described as founding a new corporation in 1998.

## Freedom Bank Kazakhstan

- https://ir.freedomholdingcorp.com/faq — Timur Turlov as the founder of the financial group.
- https://tt.bankffin.kz/en — Official biography identifies Freedom Finance's founding in 2008. Its public client-side bundle was readable even though the web reader did not expose the rendered page.
- https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924805/000165495420008328/frhc_ex991.htm — Kassa Nova's establishment in 2009 and the 2020 acquisition agreement.
- https://bankffin.kz/storage/files/docs/351/doc-ru-351-1660558327.pdf — December 2020 acquisition completion and the legal name change on 1 February 2021.
- https://bankffin.kz/ru/articles/739-ao-bank-fridom-finans-kazaxstan-smenilo-nazvanie — Announcement dated 24 May 2024 and re-registration on 20 May.
- https://bankffin.kz/en/about — Retail, business and digital services.
- Turlov is described as the founder of Freedom Finance and the wider group. The separate history of the banking entity as Kassa Nova is stated explicitly. No unsupported original-bank founder is asserted.

## Administration

`FOUNDER-A`, `FOUNDER-B`, `HISTORY-A`, `HISTORY-B`, `SERVICES-A`, and `SERVICES-B` use the same protected record editor and persistent D1 storage as the other portfolio records. History paragraphs beginning with a year and an em dash render as a timeline. The most recent image attached to a company or founder record is used for its photograph. Supporting documents remain available through the original document and evidence flows.

The shared browser-control tool remains unavailable in this environment. Source images were inspected locally, and content, media delivery and administrator operations are checked through the Worker tests and live HTTP checks. Full browser rendering is unverified.
